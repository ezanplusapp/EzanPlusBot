TikTok API Integration Details

Review of the exact scopes requested from TikTok for Developers and their dedicated operational necessity for the @ezanplus brand channel.

Requested Scope Operational Purpose Data Handling & Privacy
user.info.basic
Required 		Verifies that the connected channel corresponds to our official verified brand handle (@ezanplus) and retrieves the creator's avatar and open ID. No end-user personal data is collected. Only the authenticated brand creator token is securely stored for scheduled publishing.
video.upload
Required 		Permits our media studio backend to initialize chunked video byte transfers to TikTok's official storage endpoints (/v2/post/publish/video/init/). Transfers strictly self-produced 1080x1920 MP4 religious educational videos complying 100% with TikTok Community Guidelines.
video.publish
Required 		Enables direct, scheduled publication (Direct Post) to our official TikTok profile stream without requiring manual device handoff for every post. Zero automated spam. Publishing follows a strict schedule (2-3 daily educational and reflective posts) pre-approved by our editorial team.
